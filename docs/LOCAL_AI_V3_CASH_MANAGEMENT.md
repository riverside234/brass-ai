The reported player continued taking turns after running out of money. This repair addresses cash management; the game still has four players. Zero cash alone is legal and is not evidence that a player has been eliminated.

## Reproduced defect

In a four-player Canal position with one action remaining, £10 cash and income of −£10, Balanced v2 chose a £7 brewery. The completed turn left £3 against a £10 income payment, creating a £7 shortfall. Conserving the payment was legal. The previous evaluator credited uncertain future flips and investment value without giving the imminent payment priority. Its greedy incumbent could also make this mistake if search ran out of time.

V3 changes complete-turn selection for all six strategies:

- Compute the next income payment from the candidate's **actual** cash and income, after both actions and all resource costs. Possible opponent consumption or future sales cannot pay this bill.
- Prefer a complete plan that covers the payment. If every examined plan has a shortfall, prefer the smallest shortfall. Apply the existing bounded personality preferences within that affordable set.
- Before publishing an unsafe initial incumbent, try bounded recovery plans using legal loans, immediate sales and passes. A sale can unlock a previously unavailable loan for the second action. This protection also runs when the ordinary thinking budget has expired; its bounded completion overrun is still reported.
- When income is below −£7 and another loan is unavailable, apply a soft reserve preference for the following payment plus £7 of working capital. It encourages recovery without requiring a fixed cash balance in every position.
- Respect the existing rules: final Rail income is not collected; final introductory Canal income is collected. Exact terminal win share and rank retain priority over heuristic cash preferences.

The reserve preference is a heuristic, not a proof of future solvency. Opponents, resource competition, an already distressed position or poor earlier investments can still cause liquidation. A player may also finish an affordable plan with £0. No money is granted, no debt is waived, and no player is removed.
